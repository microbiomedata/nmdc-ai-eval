Study: Seasonal stratification and microbial community turnover in a eutrophic lake water column
Description: Water was collected monthly from May to October 2021 at the deepest point of a eutrophic lake in Dane County, Wisconsin, USA, from the surface (0.5 m) and from 20 m, using a Van Dorn sampler. Temperature and dissolved oxygen were recorded with a sonde at each depth; in August the 20 m sample was anoxic (0.1 mg per liter dissolved oxygen) at 8.4 degrees Celsius while the surface was 24.1 degrees Celsius. Two liters were filtered through 0.22 micrometer polyethersulfone filters for DNA extraction.

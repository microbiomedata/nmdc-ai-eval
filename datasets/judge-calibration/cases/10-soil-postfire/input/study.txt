Study: Soil microbial recovery after high-severity wildfire and salvage logging in a Cascade Range conifer forest
Description: Mineral soil (0-5 cm, after removing the ash layer) was sampled in June 2023 at 24 plots in a national forest in Douglas County, Oregon, USA, two years after a 2021 wildfire. Plots were in three conditions: high soil burn severity with salvage logging, high soil burn severity without logging, and unburned reference stands of Douglas-fir. Slopes ranged from 10 to 35 percent. Sample names encode the plot condition (HS-logged, HS-unlogged or unburned) and the plot number.

Study: Lignocellulose degradation by hindgut communities of a wood-feeding termite
Description: Worker termites of Reticulitermes flavipes were collected from a decaying oak log in a hardwood forest in Athens, Georgia, USA, in May 2022, and kept in the laboratory on a diet of pine wood shavings for 14 days before dissection. Whole hindguts were pooled, 20 per sample, and stored in RNAlater at -80 degrees Celsius.

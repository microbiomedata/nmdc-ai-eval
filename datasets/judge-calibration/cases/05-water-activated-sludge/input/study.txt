Study: Nitrogen removal performance and microbial community of a municipal activated sludge plant across seasons
Description: Mixed liquor was grab-sampled from the aeration basin and the anoxic zone of a municipal wastewater treatment plant in Ann Arbor, Michigan, USA, every two weeks from January to December 2023. Dissolved oxygen was 2.0 mg per liter in the aeration basin and 0.1 mg per liter in the anoxic zone. Influent ammonium averaged 35 mg N per liter over the year. Samples were centrifuged and the pellets frozen at -80 degrees Celsius. Sample names encode the zone (aer for the aeration basin, anox for the anoxic zone) and the sampling date.

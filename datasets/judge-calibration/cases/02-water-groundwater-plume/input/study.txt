Study: Nitrate and uranium attenuation by groundwater microbial communities at a legacy waste site
Description: Groundwater was pumped from six monitoring wells in a valley near Oak Ridge, Tennessee, USA, in March 2024 after purging three well volumes, using a peristaltic pump, and filtered on site through 0.2 micrometer filters. Wells W106 and W021 are within the contaminant plume (pH 3.6 and nitrate 9,800 mg per liter at W106) and wells W301 and W305 are upgradient background wells (pH 6.9 and nitrate below 1 mg per liter). Screened intervals were 7 to 12 m below ground surface.

Study: Nitrate and uranium attenuation by groundwater microbial communities at a legacy waste site
Description: Groundwater was pumped from six monitoring wells in a valley near Oak Ridge, Tennessee, USA, in March 2024 after purging three well volumes, using a peristaltic pump, and filtered on site through 0.2 micrometer filters. Wells W106 and W021 are within the contaminant plume; at W106 the pH was 3.6 and nitrate 9,800 mg per liter. Wells W301 and W305 are upgradient background wells; at W301 the pH was 6.9 and nitrate 0.4 mg per liter. Sample names give the well and the sampling month. Screened intervals were 7 to 12 m below ground surface.

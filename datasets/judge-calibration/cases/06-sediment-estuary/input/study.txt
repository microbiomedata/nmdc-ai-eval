Study: Sulfur cycling microbes in estuarine sediments along a salinity gradient
Description: Sediment cores (30 cm) were collected in June 2022 from three sites in a Chesapeake Bay tributary, Maryland, USA, by push coring from a small boat. Bottom-water salinity was 5 practical salinity units at the upper site and 18 at the lower site. Cores were sliced at 0-2 cm and 10-12 cm. The surface slices were brown and oxic; the deeper slices were black, sulfidic and anoxic. Total organic carbon ranged from 1.2 to 3.4 percent dry weight across all slices.

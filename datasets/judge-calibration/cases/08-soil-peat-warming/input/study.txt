Study: Warming and elevated CO2 effects on peat microbial communities in a northern bog
Description: Peat cores were collected in August 2022 from an ombrotrophic bog near Grand Rapids, Minnesota, USA, inside open-top enclosures held at +0, +4.5 and +9 degrees Celsius above ambient air temperature, with or without elevated atmospheric CO2 at 900 ppm. Cores were sectioned at 0-10 cm and 10-20 cm. Peat pH was between 3.8 and 4.2 across cores. Samples were frozen at -80 degrees Celsius within two hours of collection.

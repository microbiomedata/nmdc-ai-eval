Study: Drought responses of the root microbiome in two Populus trichocarpa genotypes
Description: Cuttings of two P. trichocarpa genotypes, BESC-97 (drought tolerant) and GW-9947 (drought susceptible), were grown in a greenhouse in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, USA, in a 1:1 mix of field soil and sand. After eight weeks, half of the plants were held at 30 percent of field capacity for 21 days while controls were watered to field capacity every two days. Rhizosphere soil and root endosphere were sampled from each plant in September 2023. Sample names encode the genotype, the watering treatment (drought or control) and the compartment (rhizo or root).
